Where else on Earth does your coastline exist?

Drop a pin on your home. A square of coastline around it is compared with every coast on the planet, rotated, mirrored, and resized, and the best overlays are ranked. Wherever your dot lands on a match is where you would "move" to. The search runs on this machine at full resolution.
